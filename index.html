FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are needed for Income Tax Return filing? +
For salaried individuals: Form 16, bank statements, investment proofs, and PAN card. For businesses: P&L statement, balance sheet, and GST returns. Our team sends you a complete personalised checklist once you connect.
How long does GST registration take? +
Typically 3–7 working days after all required documents are submitted. We handle the entire process end-to-end with no hassle to you.
Do you serve NRI clients for Indian tax filing? +
Yes. We offer complete NRI taxation services including ITR filing, Form 15CA/15CB for fund repatriation, FEMA compliance, and DTAA advisory — all handled remotely.
What is the due date for ITR filing for individuals? +
For most individuals the last date is July 31st of the assessment year. For businesses requiring audit it is October 31st. We proactively remind all our clients.
Can you help with company or LLP registration? +
Yes. We assist with Private Limited Company, LLP, OPC, and Partnership registration including all MCA filings, PAN, TAN, and GST registration.
Do you handle income tax notices and scrutiny cases? +
Yes. We provide full support for income tax notices, scrutiny assessments, and appeals before CIT(A) and ITAT.
Are your services available outside Hyderabad? +
Yes. We serve clients PAN India through our digital-first approach. All documentation can be handled remotely and securely.
How do I get started with NTR & CO LLP? +
Simply fill the contact form, call us, or message on WhatsApp. Our team responds within 1 business day and will guide you from the very first step.
